Frequently asked questions

How do I install Starcat Direct?

Download the Direct version from the homepage, open the DMG, and drag Starcat into Applications. The Direct download requires macOS 15 or later and an Apple Silicon Mac. See the installation guide for details.

How do I update Starcat?

The Direct version uses Sparkle for in-app updates. You can check for updates from Settings or the Actions menu. See the release notes for what's changed.

How do I activate or restore my Direct license?

Open Starcat's Settings → Pro → License to check or activate your Direct license. If activation fails, verify that you are using the Direct version and contact support with your purchase email and the error message. Please don't post your license key publicly. See the license guide.

How do I move my license to a new Mac?

Check the device list under Settings → Pro → License. If you have reached the device limit, unlink the old Mac to free a seat before activating the new one. See the restore guide and back up your local data before moving.

Can I use an App Store purchase in the Direct version?

App Store purchases and Direct licenses are managed separately. To restore a purchase, use the same distribution channel where you bought it. Contact us if you're unsure which version you have.

What to include when asking for help

  • Starcat version, distribution channel, macOS version, and Mac model or chip.
  • What you expected, what happened, steps to reproduce, and any error message.
  • For purchase or license questions, your purchase email; send it privately by email.

Protect your data: remove tokens, license keys, private repository details, and other sensitive information before sharing screenshots or diagnostics.
